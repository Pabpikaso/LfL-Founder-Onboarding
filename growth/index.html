Local Journey

Turn First-Time Guests into Lifelong Regulars.

Most traditional loyalty programs fail because asking a customer for 10 stamps feels like work. Our 5-Visit Local Journey uses proven behavioral science to guide new guests step-by-step into becoming regular patrons — tracked automatically by the app.

The 5-Visit Habit Path
1

Visit 1: 50% OFF (The Spark)

Why it works: Removes all hesitation for a new guest to try your space for the first time. Because the discount gets them through the door, guests naturally spend more on drinks, sides, and appetizers, driving up their total bill.

2

Visit 2: Free Drink or Side (The Momentum)

Why it works: Capitalizes on their fresh memory of Visit 1. A quick, low-cost perk encourages them to return within 7 to 14 days without hurting your food margins.

3

Visit 3: Surprise Chef's Treat (The Hook)

Why it works: Variable and unexpected rewards trigger excitement. A surprise off-menu item, dessert, or sample transforms a casual trip into an automatic habit.

4

Visit 4: 5% OFF Group Bill (The Social Anchor)

Why it works: Encourages your growing regular to bring friends, family, or coworkers. A simple 5% group discount gives them social currency to host their group at your spot, filling more seats while keeping your margins completely safe.

5

Visit 5: VIP Local Status (The Regular)

Why it works: Shifts the dynamic from discounts to status. Unlocking "Local Regular" privileges — like secret menu access, priority seating, or special birthday perks — locks in long-term emotional loyalty.

Good to know

Questions worth answering upfront.

How much does it cost, and what's included?

Your core membership — a Professional Business Profile, Full Platform Visibility, the Local Journey, the Local Gift Pass, and your own Business Portal App — is included as a Founding Partner at no cost. If you want consistent social content, Founders Table access, business trainings, and the wider founder community, you can level up to the Founders Pass for ₱8,888/year (about ₱740/month).

Do I have to give a discount?

Your 5-Visit Local Journey opens with a 50% off first visit — a proven hook designed to get new guests through the door risk-free. From there, the path shifts to low-cost perks, surprises, and status rewards instead of straight discounts, so your margins stay protected as guests become regulars.

How many businesses are you accepting?

We're building a curated Founding Circle across Southeast Asia, capped at 100 Founding Partners per city — not an open marketplace. Our Growth Managers personally visit and vet every business before inviting them in.

Do I have to manage the Local Gift Pass myself?

No. It's network-wide — you just accept and redeem it like any other purchase when a member uses one at your business. There's nothing to set up or sell.

Can I customize my 5-Visit Local Journey?

Your Growth Manager sets you up with the proven 5-Visit path by default — it's built on behavioral science to turn first-timers into regulars. If you'd like to adjust a specific reward, talk to your Growth Manager and they'll help you tune it for your business.

Is this like Groupon or a deal site?

No. There's no per-transaction commission and no race-to-the-bottom discounting. It's a membership built around your story and your community, not a flash sale.

How do I get started?

Apply at apply.localsforlocals.ph, or if a Growth Manager has already visited your business, ask them to send you your application link directly.
