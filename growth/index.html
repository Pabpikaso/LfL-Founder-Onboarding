Good to know

Questions worth answering upfront.

How much does it cost, and what's included?

Regularly ₱1,888/month, or ₱12,000/year if you pay annually — instead of paying for marketing on your own, you hand that budget to us. As a Founding Partner, the annual rate drops to ₱8,888, covering your business profile, three founder story videos, your Local Privilege, your full Business Portal — Local Gift Pass, Local Journey, and analytics — access to the Founders Table, business trainings, and visibility to every Locals for Locals member.

Do I have to give a discount?

No. Your Local Privilege can be a discount, but it can also be a complimentary item, an upgrade, or an experience — whatever protects your margins while still feeling generous.

How many businesses are you accepting?

We're building a curated Founding Circle across Southeast Asia, capped at 100 Founding Partners per city — not an open marketplace. Our Growth Managers personally visit and vet every business before inviting them in.

Do I have to manage the Local Gift Pass myself?

No. It's network-wide — you just accept and redeem it like a Local Privilege when a member uses one at your business. There's nothing to set up or sell.

What if I want to change my Local Privilege later?

Your Growth Manager can help you adjust it anytime — the goal is a perk that keeps working for your business, not one locked in forever.

Is this like Groupon or a deal site?

No. There's no per-transaction commission and no race-to-the-bottom discounting. It's an annual membership built around your story and your community, not a flash sale.

How do I get started?

Apply at apply.localsforlocals.ph, or if a Growth Manager has already visited your business, ask them to send you your application link directly.
