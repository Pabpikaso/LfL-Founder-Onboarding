FAQ

Good to know

What exactly is Locals for Locals?

A curated network connecting independent local businesses with members who want to discover and support them — through real profiles, founder stories, and member perks, not blanket discount codes.

Who can apply to be a Founding Partner?

Independent businesses in Davao, Manila, or Cebu that locals already love recommending. Every application is reviewed — this isn't an open signup.

What does Founding Circle membership cost?

₱7,000/year, which includes your business profile, founder story videos, Founders Table access, and business trainings. It's framed as joining a circle, not buying a listing.

What happens after I apply?

You'll build your profile — founder story, business details, photos, and your own Local Privilege — then our team reviews it before you're confirmed as a Founding Partner.

Is there a limit on how many businesses can join?

Yes — 100 Founding Partner spots per city. Once a city is full, new applicants join a waitlist and are contacted the moment a spot opens.

Ready to be one of the first?

Founding Circle applications are open now in Davao, Manila, and Cebu — and capped for a reason.
